課堂須知 Course Guidelines

適用於系上大一至大四各課程，包含全英語授課（EMI）班級。內容涵蓋課堂規範、口頭報告與書面報告三大部分，請於學期初詳閱，並於課程進行中持續遵循。

Applies to all department courses from freshman through senior year, including English-Medium Instruction (EMI) classes. Covers three areas — classroom conduct, oral presentations, and written reports. Please read carefully at the start of the semester and follow throughout the course.

01

課堂規範Classroom Conduct

維持專業且尊重的學習環境，是每位餐旅暨會展行銷管理系學生的基本素養。

Maintaining a professional, respectful learning environment is a baseline expectation for every student in the department.

上課請將手機靜音Silence phones in class
教室內禁止飲食No eating or drinking
電腦僅限課程用途Laptops for coursework only
  • 1 課前先預習指定章節與案例。 Preview the assigned chapters and case studies before each class.
  • 2 必讀每節課準時出席是基本義務；缺課累計達全學期三分之一，依學校規定辦理。 KEYPunctual attendance every session is required. Absences totaling one-third of the semester will be handled per university regulations.
  • 3 無法出席者須依規定完成請假手續，未依規定辦理者以曠課處理。 Students unable to attend must submit a leave request through the proper process; unexcused absences are recorded as truancy.
  • 4 出席、課堂參與與平時作業，均列入平時成績計算。 Attendance, in-class participation, and assignments all factor into the course participation grade.
  • 5 手機或電腦不得從事與課程無關之活動；教學使用、案例搜尋、AI 工具與問卷活動除外。 Phones and laptops may not be used for non-course activities; exceptions apply for instructional use, case research, AI tools, and survey activities.
  • 6 教室內不得進食，飲料以飲水為原則。 No eating in the classroom; water is the only beverage permitted.
02

口頭報告Oral Presentation

口頭報告是訓練專業表達與臨場應對能力的重要環節，全英班級請以全程英語進行報告與問答。

Oral presentations build professional delivery and on-the-spot response skills. EMI sections must present and field questions entirely in English.

時間Duration 每組 20 至 25 分鐘。20–25 minutes per group.
簡報檔Slide deck 需製作具專業程度的簡報檔案。A professional-quality slide deck is required.
上台人員Presenters 每位組員皆須上台報告，不可缺席分工。Every group member must take a speaking turn — no one presents by proxy.
報告方式Delivery style 避免逐字閱讀講稿，以口語化、有互動的方式呈現。Avoid reading verbatim from a script; present conversationally and stay engaged with the audience.
服裝Attire 必讀須著正式或專業服裝上台。KEYFormal or professional attire is required.
媒材工具Media & tools 可搭配影片、海報、AI 影音媒體或互動工具豐富呈現方式。Videos, posters, AI-generated media, or interactive tools are welcome to enrich the presentation.
提問義務Q&A duty 台下同學須於報告後提出與內容相關之問題；每位同學於學期結束前至少提問一次。Classmates must ask a content-related question after each presentation; every student must ask at least one question by semester's end.
03

書面報告Written Report

格式規範適用於所有書面報告，包含個案分析、期末報告等各類書面作業。

These formatting standards apply to all written assignments, including case analyses and final reports.

頁數Length 不限頁數。No page limit.
封面資訊Cover page 組別、成員姓名與學號、個案名稱、報告日期。Group number, member names & student IDs, case name, and submission date.
版面邊界Margins 上下左右各 2.5 公分。2.5 cm on all four sides.
字型規格Typeface 必讀中文：明體／楷書／黑體；英文與數字：Times New Roman／Arial；本文字級 12pt；行距建議 1.2。KEYChinese: Ming, Kai, or Hei typeface. English & numerals: Times New Roman or Arial. Body text 12pt, line spacing 1.2 recommended.
資料引用Citations 引用資料須標示來源，圖表亦同。All cited material, including figures and tables, must be properly sourced.
AI 使用Use of AI 使用生成式 AI 時，須自行查核內容正確性並說明使用方式。When using generative AI, verify the accuracy of the output yourself and disclose how it was used.
品質要求Quality bar 不得有明顯錯別字、資料錯誤或未經查證之敘述。No obvious typos, factual errors, or unverified claims.
提醒：以上規範自即日起適用於本系大一至大四各年級課程；全英教學（EMI）班級之口頭與書面報告，一律以英文完成，格式規範不變。如有課程個別調整，將於各課程大綱中另行說明。 Note: These guidelines apply to all department courses from freshman through senior year, effective immediately. For EMI sections, both oral and written reports must be completed entirely in English; formatting standards remain the same. Any course-specific adjustments will be noted in the individual course syllabus.
